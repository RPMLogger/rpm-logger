You do not fit students into your life.
Students pick from your menu.
Asking everyone for all their free times and solving the puzzle is what made this feel like too much. The grid below is the whole supply. Everything outside it is not for sale.

21 slots a week. Mon, Wed and Sun are the 4-in-a-row days. Tue, Thu, Fri are 3. That question is answered. Do not reopen it. (Sun 1:30 was added for Cathy, Sep 2026, because it glued onto the end of the block with no gap. That is the only way a new hour ever gets added.)
Weekly student needs a slot empty in BOTH weeks
openThu 6:30makes Thursday a clean 3 in a row
openFri 8:30makes Friday a clean 3 in a row
openWed 8:30Wednesday goes to 4, it is the long day
openMon 5:30last, gap at 4:30 in B week until Jean Gabriel gets a partner
Offer the top two. If neither works, the third. If none work, waitlist. Never invent a new hour.
Biweekly student goes into a half-empty slot, costs zero new hours
B weekMon 4:30pairs with Jean Gabriel (A)
B weekTue 6:30pairs with Yaxu (A)
B weekWed 7:30pairs with Charlotte (A)
A weekSun 10:30pairs with Flora (B)
A weekTue 8:30pairs with Jiayi (B)

These six halves are reserved for biweekly only. A weekly student can never have one. Do not give a biweekly student a fully open slot while a half is still empty.
End of the trial ask this in the room, saves three texts
Look at this tab before the trial. Know the three weekly spots and the three biweekly halves before you walk in. The grid only protects you if you say it out loud.
The commitment still happens later, alone, after the terms. Asking weekly or biweekly and which slot is logistics, not selling.
Pencilled, not booked. The spot is theirs once the form comes back. If another form lands first, they get the next option.
SayHere's how I do it. Don't decide right now. I'll email you my terms tonight, read them, sleep on it, and if you're in, there's a short form to acknowledge them. That's your yes. But so we don't go back and forth later, are you thinking weekly or biweekly? ... Okay, for weekly I have ___, ___, or ___. Which would you want? ... I'll pencil that in. It's yours once the form comes back.
Text that eveningHey ___! Really enjoyed today, I'd be happy to keep going with you. Just emailed the homework, the Dropbox instructions, the terms, and a short form. I've pencilled you in for ___ at ___ weekly. If the form comes back, that spot is yours. If not, no worries at all, no need to explain anything, we're all good.
The form is the yes, not a text back. Silence is a no, and silence is fine. Asking weekly or biweekly before they see the four-at-once term costs nothing: anyone who flips after reading it was a biweekly student anyway.
The script
WeeklyHey ___! Great, glad to have you on board. For weekly lessons I have Thursday at 6:30pm, Friday at 8:30pm, or Wednesday at 8:30pm open right now. Which one works best for you?
BiweeklyHey ___! For biweekly lessons I have Tuesday at 6:30pm, Wednesday at 7:30pm, or Sunday at 10:30am open right now. Which one works best for you?
Nothing fitsNo problem. Those are the only spots I have open at the moment. I can put you first in line for the next one that opens up, if you would like.
Never
Ask "when are you free?" You offer, they pick.
Offer a time outside the grid. Not even once, not even for someone you really want.
Move an existing student to make room. That is the "too much" from before.
Push a weekly student to biweekly to fill a half. A weekly student is worth two biweekly students.
Fill a departure by opening a new hour. The slot that just emptied goes to the next person in line.
How it tightens by itself
Every new student lands in the right spot, so the grid only ever gets tighter.
When someone leaves, their slot goes to the waitlist, never a new hour.
When a student asks to move on their own, offer them the tightest option.
Six months of this beats any reshuffle you could do by hand, with zero awkward texts. The only manual move worth making: one student holding an ugly gap open, and a better slot already sitting empty for them.
This page is a snapshot. When a slot fills, cross it off here. When the grid changes, update the grid.
